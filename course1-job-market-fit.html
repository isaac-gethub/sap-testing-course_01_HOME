Designed With Intent

Built to match what the job actually requires — entry-level testing through manager-level governance.

The Full Sweep was designed around one goal: give a trainee everything a live SAP SOX/ITGC or GRC posting actually asks for, across the full career ladder — not just the tasks a junior analyst handles on day one.

  • Every control domain a posting names has its own Book. Access, change, computer operations, GRC platform modules, business application controls, Order-to-Cash, and Treasury & Tax each get full execution steps and a completed evidence exhibit — not a summary paragraph.
  • Manager-level competencies are included, not assumed. RCM construction, design-versus-operating effectiveness, sampling rationale, and deficiency classification each get their own Book in the Manager Track — the exact skills postings expect once a role moves beyond pure testing.
  • Emerging 2026 themes are covered directly. Clean Core/RISE governance — extensibility classification, transport governance, and reviewing AI-generated code — has its own dedicated Book, matching one of the fastest-growing requirements in current postings.
  • Tools are named specifically, not described generically. Pathlock, Fastpath, and AuditBoard each appear by name, the same way a job posting names them.
Where We're Adding Next

Sharpening how we talk about what's already there.

Three specific items are already in progress:

  • An "IPE validation" glossary note. The course already teaches the underlying discipline through its evidence/exhibit standard — the term itself just isn't named yet, and it shows up as an explicit line item in postings.
  • Region-specific messaging. UK/Europe postings often pair SAP FICO + SOX with Computer System Validation and a chartered-accountant credential; UAE/Middle East postings skew toward build/configure work over testing/audit. The course content already supports both — the marketing hasn't caught up to say so explicitly yet.
  • A certification cross-reference. A one-page map from course Books to CISA/CRISC exam domains — no new content, just a guide for trainees already pursuing those credentials alongside the course.

What we deliberately left out

Two things came up in this search that we're not chasing: a single mention of SAP Ariba/SuccessFactors scope expansion at one company (not a recurring pattern), and general InfoSec GRC frameworks like ISO 27001, NIST, and SOC 2 that show up in postings blending SAP-specific and general cybersecurity responsibilities. Both belong to a different job family than the one this course targets — pulling them in would dilute the course rather than strengthen it.
