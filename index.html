The 11 Parts, at a Glance
  • PART I — Access Control & Identity

    The domain most job postings name directly — access risk, SoD conflicts, and joiner/mover/leaver testing.

  • PART II — Change & Operations

    The second most-named domain — change management, backups, interfaces, and code-level access risk.

  • PART III — Record to Report

    Journal-entry approvals, post-acquisition testing, and investigation-driven sampling — where the numbers get tested.

  • PART IV — SAP GRC Platform Modules

    Testing the automated controls tools themselves — a level above testing the controls they monitor.

  • PART V — Governance & Disclosure

    How testing work becomes a CFO's signature and an SEC response — governance and disclosure timing.

  • PART VI — Legal & Compliance Adjacent

    Data retention and software licensing exposure — compliance risk just outside classic ITGC scope.

  • PART VII — Business Application Controls

    Matching, payments, tolerances, and batch jobs — the business-process control layer most courses skip.

  • PART VIII — Order-to-Cash Controls

    Credit-block release independence and billing cutoff — the revenue side, held to the same standard as Part VII.

  • PART IX — Treasury & Tax Controls

    Bank reconciliation aging and tax configuration change testing — exposure most junior testers never get assigned.

  • PART X — Adjacent Tools & Emerging Practice

    Third-party GRC platforms, full-population data analytics, and GRC 2026/AI-assisted review.

  • PART XI — Manager Track Elective

    RCM construction, design effectiveness, sampling, and deficiency classification — from finding to closure.

Every single class

Every one of the 20 live sessions runs the same two-hour structure — no session is theory-only, no session is a passive watch.

Hour 1 — Theory

The scenario, the control objective, the pitfall.

The instructor walks that day's Book(s): the story, the control being tested, the real-world mistake it's built around, and what "good" evidence looks like — before anyone touches the system.

Hour 2 — Demo Lab

Live, on a real system, screen-shared.

The instructor runs the actual T-codes and Fiori apps for that day's topic on a live S/4HANA training client — the same screens you'll repeat yourself before the next class.

Independent repetition is mandatory, not optional homework. Every student repeats that day's full transaction walkthrough on their own training-client login before the next session — the instructor spot-checks evidence at the start of the following class.
