Assessment & Format

The Full Sweep, one company, built the way a real engagement runs.

A live, instructor-led cohort — not a self-paced library. You move through the Controls Theory prerequisite, the optional Foundations, and the 11 Parts across 20 live sessions with a real instructor, with the Live SAP window open every session so you execute what you're being taught immediately, not days or weeks later.

What's included

Nothing metered, nothing gated behind an upsell

  • The Controls Theory prerequisite — 11 topics (B0–B10) plus a 49-question final exam, 60% to pass, before Book 1 starts
  • 8 optional Foundations modules — pure SAP navigation for anyone without prior hands-on experience
  • 35 core Books across Parts I–X, plus 4 Manager Track Elective Books in Part XI — 39 Books total across all 11 Parts, the complete Thornfield Industrial Group case
  • Every Book — all 39 — comes with a fully completed workpaper exhibit: a real finished example to check your own work against, not a blank template
  • A Live SAP window running side-by-side with every topic — the same S/4HANA session, transaction codes, and Fiori apps a working consultant uses
  • The Evidence & Templates Library — the control policy, control matrix, evidence tracker, and entity-level controls register a real engagement runs on
What a reviewer actually checks

SAP execution is the easy part. This is what every completed exhibit models.

Every Book's fully completed workpaper exhibit is built to show you the same seven things a reviewer looks for on a real engagement — not just whether you clicked the right transaction.

  • 1Business risk & control understanding — can you say why this control exists before you test it
  • 2Testing strategy & population definition — did you request the full population, or accept a pre-selected sample
  • 3Evidence evaluation — does the evidence actually support the conclusion, or just look complete
  • 4Consultant judgement & escalation — do you know when a finding needs to go up, not just get logged
  • 5Workpaper & conclusion quality — would this survive a reviewer's first pass without a follow-up question
  • 6Stakeholder communication — could you explain this finding to the SAP team and the audit team, in each one's own language
  • 7SAP execution accuracy — the right transaction, the right screen, correctly documented

If you only ever get faster at clicking through transactions, execution accuracy is the one line on this list you'll improve — and the one that matters least once you're actually staffed on an engagement.

How it runs

Theory, then live system, every single session.

The order it runs in

  • Controls Theory prerequisite (B0–B10) and the 49-question exam — before Book 1, Days 1–2
  • Foundations (101–108) — for anyone without prior hands-on SAP time
  • Parts I through X, in order, across 20 live sessions — each one Hour 1 Theory, Hour 2 live Demo Lab
  • The Manager Track Elective (Part XI) — self-paced, after the live cohort ends

What's required between sessions

  • Independent repetition of that day's full transaction walkthrough on your own training-client login
  • The instructor spot-checks evidence at the start of the following class
  • Self-check against each Book's completed exhibit before your own evidence goes in the app
